Absolutely. Since today you're focusing on **conditional statements (`if`, `else if`, `else`, nested `if`, logical operators)**, here are 10 practice questions in increasing difficulty.

### 🟢 Easy — 1–3

**1. Voting Eligibility**
Take a person's age as input.

* If age ≥ 18 → print `"Eligible to vote"`
* Otherwise → print `"Not eligible to vote"`

**2. Positive, Negative or Zero**
Take a number as input and print whether it is:

* Positive
* Negative
* Zero

**3. Even or Odd**
Take an integer as input and print whether it is **Even** or **Odd**.

---

### 🟡 Medium — 4–7

**4. Find the Greatest of Two Numbers**
Take two numbers as input and print the greater number.
If both are equal, print `"Both are equal"`.

**5. Student Grade**
Take marks as input and print the grade:

* `90–100` → A
* `80–89` → B
* `70–79` → C
* `60–69` → D
* Below 60 → F

**6. Leap Year**
Take a year as input and determine whether it is a leap year.

**7. Electricity Bill**
Take units consumed as input:

* Up to 100 units → ₹5/unit
* 101–200 → ₹7/unit
* Above 200 → ₹10/unit

Calculate and print the total bill.

---

### 🔴 Hard — 8–10

**8. Find the Greatest of Three Numbers**
Take three numbers as input and print the greatest number.

Example:

```text
Input: 25 78 43
Output: 78 is the greatest
``` 

**9. Student Result System**
Take marks of **5 subjects**.

Calculate:

* Total marks
* Percentage
* Result

Rules:

* If **any subject < 33** → `"Fail"`
* Otherwise percentage ≥ 75 → `"Distinction"`
* 60–74 → `"First Division"`
* 50–59 → `"Second Division"`
* 33–49 → `"Pass"`

**10. ATM Withdrawal 💳**
Take:

* Account balance
* Withdrawal amount

Rules:

* Withdrawal must be a multiple of ₹100
* Withdrawal amount must not exceed balance
* Minimum balance after withdrawal must be ₹500

Print:

* `"Withdrawal successful"` if all conditions are satisfied
* Otherwise print the appropriate reason.

**Today's challenge:** Try solving **1 → 7 without help**, then attempt **8 → 10**. Don't use loops or arrays yet—solve everything using conditional statements only.
